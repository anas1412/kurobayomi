# M03 — Local reading and playing

Slug: `M03-local-reading-and-playing`

Goal: Local source for manga and anime, image pipeline and cover cache, reader core and viewers, player core and controls on libmpv.

Exit criteria:
- you can open a local CBZ or folder and page through it
- you can play a local video file with mpv and your position is saved
